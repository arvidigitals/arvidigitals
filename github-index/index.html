    About ARVI Digitals

    Established on 5 February 2023, ARVI Digitals is a Salem-based creative studio bringing photography, advertising and digital marketing together under one roof.

    With 60+ clients served, we have worked across different creative projects, helping individuals capture important occasions and businesses establish a strong visual presence.

    We offer a range of services, including wedding and event photography, portraits, model portfolios, commercial photography, advertising campaigns, brand identity design, content creation and digital marketing. Every service is designed to meet different creative and business requirements.

    We believe good visuals do more than attract attention. They communicate ideas, represent identities and create meaningful connections. That's why we focus on delivering practical creative solutions that help our clients present their best.
